Everything you need to know before funding your account & trading live.

Some networks require a minimum deposit threshold. We accept deposits in major assets only: USDT (TRC-20, ERC-20, BEP-20), Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, and USDC. Please do not send unsupported assets as they cannot be recovered.

Asset Network Minimum Deposit
USDT TRC-20 (TRON) 10 USDT
USDT ERC-20 (ETH) 50 USDT
Bitcoin BTC Native 0.00025 BTC
Solana SOL Native 0.1 SOL
Ethereum ERC-20 (ETH) 0.01 ETH
Important: Deposits sent below the minimum threshold or to an incorrect network address cannot be credited or recovered. Always double-check contract details prior to transferring.

All incoming crypto transactions are credited automatically as soon as they achieve required blockchain network confirmations:

  • USDT (TRC-20): < 3 minutes (19 confirmations)
  • Bitcoin: ~10 - 20 minutes (2 confirmations)
  • Solana: < 30 seconds (Finalized state)

Automated hot wallet withdrawals process instantaneously (within 5 minutes). Security checks for high-volume transactions are audited within 1-2 hours.

We mandate Two-Factor Authentication (2FA via Google Authenticator or WebAuthn keys). 98% of user funds are stored in offline multi-signature cold vaults.
